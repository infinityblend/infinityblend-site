Getting started

What is an infinity bottle?

An infinity bottle is a bottle you keep topping up with small pours from other bottles. Over time it becomes a blend that's yours alone. Infinity Blend keeps track of every pour, the blend's proof and volume, and how it tastes.

How do I start my first blend?

Open the app, create a blend, pick its bottle shape and size, then tap Add a pour. Want to look around first? Go to Settings → Try it out → Add sample blends.

Can I see millilitres or ABV instead of ounces and proof?

Yes. In Settings → Display, choose how pours are measured and turn on Show ABV instead of proof. Changing units only changes what you see. Every pour keeps its exact amount.

How does label scanning work?

When adding a pour, scan the bottle's label with the camera or pick a label photo. The text is read on your iPhone to fill in the name and proof. Scanning needs a real iPhone camera and camera permission, which you can turn on in the iPhone Settings app → Infinity Blend.

Infinity Blend Pro

What does Pro add?

Pro adds AI tasting profiles that are rewritten after every pour, and a preview of how the next pour will change your blend. The journal itself is free: unlimited bottles and pours, the layered bottle, flavour sliders, label scanning and charts.

Why don't I see AI tasting notes?

The AI runs on your iPhone using Apple Intelligence. It needs an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence, with it turned on in Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri. On other iPhones the app works normally and just hides the AI text. Settings → Apple Intelligence inside Infinity Blend shows whether it's available.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

In Infinity Blend, go to Settings → Pro → Manage subscription, or open the iPhone Settings app → your name → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Pro stays active until the end of the period you've paid for.

I subscribed, but Pro is locked (new iPhone or reinstall)

Go to Settings → Pro → Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. We never see your payment details.

Your data and privacy

Where is my journal stored?

Only on your iPhone. There's no account and no server copy of your bottles, pours or notes. Your data is included in your iPhone backups (for example iCloud Backup) if you have them turned on. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a single pour or blend in the app, delete everything in Settings → Delete all data, or delete the app. Because nothing is stored on our side, there's no account to close.

Can I move my journal to a new iPhone?

Yes. Restore your new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup of your old one, and your Infinity Blend data comes with it.

Troubleshooting

Something isn't working
  • Make sure you have the latest version of Infinity Blend and iOS.
  • Fully close the app (swipe it up in the app switcher) and open it again.
  • Still stuck? Email support@infinityblend.app with what happened and a screenshot.

Drink responsibly

Infinity Blend is for adults of legal drinking age (21+ in the United States). Proof figures are estimates. Never drink and drive. If you're worried about drinking, the SAMHSA National Helpline is free, confidential and open 24/7: 1-800-662-4357.
