Privacy Policy

Your journal stays on your iPhone. The only thing that ever leaves it is a bottle you choose to look up online. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Who we are

Infinity Blend is made by Sahar Ventures LLC, a Florida limited liability company ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with information. To reach us, email support@infinityblend.app.

The short version

Your bottles, pours, ratings and notes stay on your iPhone. We can't see them. There's no account, and we don't sell, rent or share your information. The one exception is the optional online bottle lookup: when you tap it, the bottle name, label text and label photos you chose are sent to identify that bottle, as explained below.

What the app stores on your iPhone

  • Bottles and blends you create: names, spirit type, bottle shape and size.
  • Pours: spirit names, proof, amounts, dates, colours, flavour ratings and notes.
  • Your ratings, tasting notes and settings (such as ounces or millilitres).
  • AI-written tasting summaries and tags for your blends.

This information is stored only in the app's storage on your device.

Camera and label scanning

If you choose to scan a label, the app uses your camera to read the text on the bottle. The reading happens on your iPhone. Camera images aren't saved by the app and aren't sent anywhere. You can turn camera access off at any time in the iPhone Settings app.

On-device AI

Tasting summaries, pour previews and label reading use Apple's on-device language model (Apple Intelligence) when your iPhone supports it and it's turned on. The text the app sends to the model, such as your pour list and flavour ratings, is processed on your iPhone and is not sent to us. Apple's handling of Apple Intelligence is covered by Apple's own privacy policy.

Online bottle lookup (optional)

When you tap "Find this bottle online", or scan a label while online lookup is available, the app sends: the bottle name in the name field, the text read from the label, up to three label photos (made smaller first), the spirit type you picked, and a random ID created for this install. It does not send your other bottles, pours, ratings, notes, name, location or contact details.

This information goes to our lookup service, which runs on Supabase (our hosting provider). The service passes the name, label text and photos to Anthropic's Claude API, which reads them and searches the web to identify the bottle, under Anthropic's commercial terms. We use this only to answer that lookup.

We don't keep your label photos. For typed-name lookups we save the answer (bottle facts and published tasting notes, nothing about you) for up to 30 days so the same bottle doesn't have to be searched twice. The random install ID and a count of lookups per day are kept to apply daily limits and stop abuse; the ID isn't linked to you, your Apple ID or your device's advertising identifier. We delete usage counts after about 30 days.

Lookup results are found by AI from web sources and can be wrong. Each result shows its sources so you can check it. If you never use online lookup, nothing leaves your iPhone.

What we don't do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No location, contacts or health data, and no access to your photo library. If you pick label photos from your library, the app only gets the photos you pick.
  • No sale of personal information, and no sharing of it for advertising.

Purchases

If you subscribe to Infinity Blend Pro, Apple handles the payment. We never receive your card or payment details. Apple tells the app only whether your subscription is active, so the app can unlock Pro.

Backups and sharing

Like other app data, Infinity Blend's data may be included in your iPhone backups (for example, iCloud Backup or a computer backup) if you have backups turned on. Those backups are controlled by you and Apple, not us. If you use the Share button, the text you share goes wherever you choose to send it.

Keeping or deleting your data

Your data stays until you delete it. You can delete a single pour or blend in the app, delete everything with Settings → Delete all data, or delete the app, which removes its data from your iPhone.

Age

Infinity Blend is about alcoholic drinks and is meant only for people who are of legal drinking age where they live, and at least 21 in the United States. We don't knowingly provide the app to anyone younger.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to know, access, correct or delete personal information. Everything in your journal is already on your device and under your control. The only things we hold are the anonymous lookup counts and cached bottle facts described above. To ask about them, or to have the counts for your install deleted, email support@infinityblend.app.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we'll update this policy, change the effective date and ask you to review it in the app before the change applies to you.
