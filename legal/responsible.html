Drink Responsibly

A short reminder, and where to get help.

Draft for attorney review. Not yet a final policy.

Enjoy it, don't overdo it

Infinity bottles are about savouring small pours over time. Proof numbers in the app are estimates, and cask-strength blends can be well over 100 proof, so pour accordingly.

Never drink and drive

Plan a ride before you pour. The app can't tell you whether you're safe to drive.

Getting help

If you're worried about your drinking or someone else's, the SAMHSA National Helpline is free, confidential and open 24 hours a day, 365 days a year: 1-800-662-4357 (HELP).
